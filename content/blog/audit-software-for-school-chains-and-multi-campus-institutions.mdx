## What metrics should school trust directors monitor across campuses?

> **School trust directors should monitor four core metrics across campuses: Campus Safety Index (CSI), Average Hazard Remediation Latency, Daily Transport Pre-Trip Audit Compliance, and Regulatory Statutory Expiry Adherence.**

Central education executives monitor network risk through four key indicators:
- **Campus Safety Index (%):** Normalized compliance score aggregating weekly building, laboratory, and sanitation inspections across all network schools.
- **Hazard Remediation Latency (Hours):** Average time elapsed between flagging an infrastructure defect and receiving verified photographic proof of repair.
- **Transport Pre-Trip Completion Rate (%):** Percentage of school bus fleet safety checks completed by drivers before morning student pickup routes.
- **Statutory Document Expiry Adherence:** Automated tracking of water testing certificates, fire NOCs, and building structural stability licenses across all campus properties.
