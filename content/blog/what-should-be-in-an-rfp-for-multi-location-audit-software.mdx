When enterprise retail chains, hospitality groups, healthcare networks, or franchise corporations issue a Request for Proposal (RFP) for audit management software, procurement committees often rely on generic IT templates. These generic questionnaires focus heavily on server operating systems and database schemas while omitting the operational constraints that dictate real-world field success: offline mobile synchronisation, hardware camera locks, GPS geofencing, and automated corrective action tracking.

A poorly drafted RFP results in proposals from desk-bound GRC vendors whose complex software frontline store managers cannot use on mobile devices.

[Audiment](/) is audit management software for multi-location businesses, built to run proof-based audits, verify on-site execution, and track corrective actions to resolution.

## What are the 6 essential functional modules of an audit software RFP?

> **The six essential functional modules of an audit software RFP are: mobile field execution, anti-cheating and evidence verification, dynamic scoring and analytics, closed-loop CAPA workflows, enterprise IT security, and pricing model predictability.**

```
The Enterprise Audit Software RFP Architecture:

Module 1: Mobile Field Execution
- 100% Offline capability; fast local caching; barcode & QR asset scanning.

Module 2: Anti-Cheating & Evidence Verification
- Hardware camera lock (no gallery uploads); GPS boundary checking; NTP timestamps.

Module 3: Dynamic Scoring & Analytics
- Weighted categories; automatic fail triggers; real-time multi-unit dashboards.

Module 4: Closed-Loop CAPA Workflows
- Automated task creation; SLA timers; mandatory After-photo proof; multi-tier sign-off.

Module 5: Enterprise Security & Governance
- SOC 2 Type II; AES-256 encryption; granular RBAC; SAML 2.0 Single Sign-On.

Module 6: Commercial & Pricing Model
- Transparent flat per-location pricing; unlimited users; zero photo storage overages.
```

## What vendor questions separate real field tools from desk-bound GRC software?

> **Ask vendors five revealing operational questions: Can an auditor complete an inspection in a steel warehouse with zero cellular signal? Does the software block gallery photo uploads? How long does an executive PDF take to generate? Can store managers use the app without per-seat fees? Can we modify templates without paying professional services?**

```
5 Questions That Expose Weak Vendor Architectures:

1. "Show us a live demo where you turn on Airplane Mode, complete an inspection with 10 photos,
   and reconnect. Does the sync happen automatically without data loss or duplicate entries?"
   (Desk-bound GRC tools fail this test immediately).

2. "Show us how an auditor attaches a photo. Can they select an image from their phone camera roll?
   If the answer is Yes, how do you prevent staff from uploading yesterday's photos?"
   (Real field tools lock the camera directly).

3. "If our store count stays at 50, but we want 150 shift supervisors to have logins to resolve tasks,
   will our annual contract fee increase?"
   (Per-seat vendors will reveal massive price inflation).

4. "How many seconds elapse between clicking 'Submit Audit' on a tablet and the executive PDF report
   arriving in the Regional Director's inbox?"
   (Modern systems take under 60 seconds; legacy systems take hours or manual batching).

5. "Can our internal operations coordinator create a new checklist, re-weight scores, and deploy it
   to 50 stores in 10 minutes without writing code or hiring your consultants?"
   (Rigid legacy platforms require paid professional services).
```

## How should the procurement committee score and weight vendor proposals?

> **The procurement committee should score proposals using a 100-point weighted evaluation model: 30% Mobile Usability and Anti-Cheating, 25% Closed-Loop CAPA and Automation, 20% Commercial Economics and Transparency, 15% Enterprise IT Security, and 10% Vendor References and SLA.**

```
Enterprise RFP Evaluation Scoring Weight Distribution:

+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Criteria Category                        Weight    Scoring Focus                  |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. Mobile Usability & Anti-Cheating       30%      Offline speed, camera lock, GPS|
| 2. Closed-Loop CAPA & Automation          25%      Automated tasks, Before/After  |
| 3. Commercial Model & Total Cost (TCO)    20%      Flat pricing, no hidden fees   |
| 4. IT Architecture & Security             15%      SOC 2, encryption, SSO, DPDPA  |
| 5. Vendor Experience & Reference Sites    10%      Similar multi-unit deployments |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
| Total Evaluation Score                   100%                                     |
+-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

## What RFP timeline ensures a smooth vendor selection cycle?

> **A realistic RFP timeline spans 6 to 8 weeks: RFP issuance in Week 1, vendor submissions in Week 3, product demonstrations in Week 4, shortlisting and a 2-week paid pilot in Weeks 5 to 6, and final contract execution in Week 8.**

```
The 8-Week Enterprise Procurement Roadmap:

Week 1: Issue RFP document to 4 shortlisted vendors
Week 2: Vendor query clarification window
Week 3: Formal RFP proposal submission deadline
Week 4: Live scenario-based product demonstrations (focus on mobile field workflows)
Week 5-6: 14-day paid pilot deployment across 5 real retail stores
Week 7: Commercial negotiations and Master Services Agreement (MSA) review
Week 8: Board contract sign-off and project kickoff
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Should custom API integrations be included in the primary RFP scope?</strong></summary>
<p>Yes. Request documentation for existing REST APIs and webhooks, and ask vendors to specify whether connecting to your ERP, POS, or maintenance system requires custom development fees or is supported via out-of-the-box connectors.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How many references should the procurement committee contact?</strong></summary>
<p>Demand at least 3 customer references operating in your specific industry vertical (e.g., retail chains, QSRs, or hospital networks) with a comparable location count (e.g., 30+ outlets), and interview operations managers directly regarding system adoption.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Should vendors be required to provide a sandbox trial during the RFP stage?</strong></summary>
<p>Yes. Require shortlisted vendors to provide full administrative and mobile application access to a functional sandbox environment for at least 7 days before final commercial scoring.</p>
</details>
