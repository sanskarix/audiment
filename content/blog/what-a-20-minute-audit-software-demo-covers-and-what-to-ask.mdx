When enterprise operations leaders schedule a software demonstration, vendor sales teams routinely take control: spending 15 minutes showing marketing slides, reading customer logos, and demonstrating trivial tasks like changing button colors on a desktop browser. By the time the call ends, operations directors have seen zero mobile workflows, verified no anti-cheating controls, and learned nothing about how the software behaves on frontline store floors.

A 20-minute software demo must be an active operational interrogation. Operations leaders should take control of the agenda, force the vendor onto mobile devices, and test five specific operational scenarios.

[Audiment](/) is audit management software for multi-location businesses, built to run proof-based audits, verify on-site execution, and track corrective actions to resolution.

## What is the ideal 20-minute audit software demo agenda?

> **The ideal 20-minute demo agenda allocates 2 minutes to operational requirements, 6 minutes to live mobile field execution, 6 minutes to closed-loop CAPA remediation, 4 minutes to executive dashboard rollups, and 2 minutes to commercial licensing terms.**

```
The 20-Minute Operational Demo Schedule:

Minute 0 - 2: Operational Framing
- State your exact constraints: "We operate 45 stores, employ 90 shift supervisors,
  and need to eliminate paper checklists and unverified WhatsApp photos."

Minute 2 - 8: Live Mobile Fieldwork (Screen-Shared from a Real Smartphone)
- Test mobile checklist speed, offline capability, GPS check-in, and camera locking.

Minute 8 - 14: Automated CAPA & Remediation Loop
- Flag an audit failure; watch the system generate a task; execute photo-backed closure.

Minute 14 - 18: Executive Console & Portfolio Analytics
- Review cross-store league tables, recurring defect Pareto charts, and overdue SLAs.

Minute 18 - 20: Commercial & Contract Clarity
- Confirm flat per-location pricing, unlimited user accounts, and data export terms.
```

## What 5 live operational stress-tests should you demand during the demo?

> **Demand five live stress-tests: turning on Airplane Mode to test offline data capture, attempting a photo upload from the device camera roll, triggering an automated CAPA ticket from a failed checkpoint, testing sub-50-metre GPS geofencing, and generating an executive PDF report in under 60 seconds.**

Force the vendor to execute these five real-world scenarios live on their screen:

```
5 Live Software Stress-Tests:

Test 1: The "Airplane Mode" Drop Test
Ask the presenter: "Turn on Airplane Mode on your phone right now. Answer 3 questions,
take a photo, and submit. Did the app crash, or did it queue locally and sync
automatically once reconnected?"

Test 2: The "Camera Roll Trap"
Ask: "Tap the photo attachment button. Can you select an image saved in your
phone's photo gallery? If Yes, how do you prevent staff from uploading yesterday's photos?"

Test 3: The Automated CAPA Trigger
Ask: "Mark Question #4 (Cold Room Temp) as FAIL. Show us the maintenance screen.
Did a tracked task automatically populate with an assigned owner and SLA countdown?"

Test 4: The Sub-50m Geofence Lockout
Ask: "Set a geofence for your current location. Move the device coordinates 100 metres away.
Does the app block the inspection submission, or does it accept it anyway?"

Test 5: The 60-Second PDF Challenge
Ask: "Submit that completed audit. Time how many seconds it takes for the final,
branded executive PDF report to arrive in your inbox."
```

Vendors with reliable field tools welcome these tests; vendors selling desk-bound GRC software will try to redirect the conversation back to desktop slide decks.

## What commercial and technical questions expose hidden vendor weaknesses?

> **Ask four specific questions: Are store managers charged individual seat fees? What happens to our data if we cancel? What are the monthly photo storage limits? Can our operations team edit checklist templates without paying professional services?**

| Evaluation Question | Warning Answer (Walk Away) | Reassuring Answer (Proceed) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **User Account Pricing** | "Every user who accesses the app requires a $29 monthly seat license." | "Flat per-location pricing: unlimited store managers, technicians, and auditors." |
| **Data Extraction Rights** | "Historical data can be extracted via professional services at $180/hour." | "100% free self-service export of raw CSV, JSON, and original photo archives anytime." |
| **Evidence Media Caps** | "Your plan includes 15 GB of media storage; overages are billed per gigabyte." | "Unlimited photo evidence storage included in base subscription; multi-year retention." |
| **Template Flexibility** | "Template modifications require submitting a change request to our engineering team." | "Self-service drag-and-drop template builder; update and deploy to 50 stores in 5 minutes." |

## How do you evaluate mobile app usability during the demonstration?

> **Evaluate mobile usability by watching how many screen taps are required to complete a checkpoint, whether text fields force typing instead of binary toggles, how fast photos compress, and whether questions can be toggled into regional languages.**

Frontline store associates will not use software that feels like an enterprise tax form:
- **Tap Efficiency:** A standard 20-question morning check should require fewer than 30 total screen taps.
- **Visual Reference Aids:** Check whether checklist questions display side-by-side "Golden Sample" benchmark photos so junior associates understand what "Clean" looks like.
- **Language Switching:** Ask the presenter to toggle the checklist from English to Hindi, Bahasa, or Spanish. A single-tap translation ensures frontline associates understand critical safety rules.

## What next steps should follow a successful demo?

> **Follow a successful demo by requesting a 14-day paid pilot across 5 outlets, requiring the vendor to digitize one of your actual operational checklists, and setting quantitative success benchmarks.**

Never commit to an annual 50-store contract directly from a demo. High-performing procurement teams require:
1. **A 14-Day 5-Store Pilot:** Deploy the software across 5 diverse operational outlets to validate real-world staff adoption.
2. **Template Conversion:** Require the vendor to digitize your actual master checklist into the platform within 48 hours.
3. **Written Commercial Terms:** Ensure all verbal commitments regarding unlimited users, flat per-location pricing, and included photo storage are reflected in the Master Services Agreement draft.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<details>
<summary><strong>Who from our company should attend the 20-minute software demo?</strong></summary>
<p>The call should include the VP or Director of Operations, at least one active Territory or Area Manager who conducts store visits, and an IT procurement representative to verify data security.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How long does a vendor need to prepare for a customized demo?</strong></summary>
<p>A capable software vendor requires 24 to 48 hours to ingest one of your existing PDF or Excel inspection forms and configure a working mobile demonstration tailored to your exact store layout.</p>
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Should we sign a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) before the demo?</strong></summary>
<p>Yes. If you share proprietary operational manuals, store location lists, or custom recipes for the vendor to configure sample checklists, execute a mutual NDA prior to sharing documents.</p>
</details>
