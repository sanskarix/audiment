<p>Yes. Operations teams can set score thresholds (such as any overall audit score below 80% or any critical food safety failure) that trigger instant automated alerts to the regional director, store general manager, and designated maintenance technician via WhatsApp or Slack webhooks.</p>

<summary><strong>How does the system handle historical reporting when checklist questions are updated mid-year?</strong></summary>
<p>Audiment version-controls every checklist template. When operational questions change, past completed audits remain permanently tied to the exact template version active during their execution, preserving historical scoring integrity without corrupting trend analyses.</p>
</details>
