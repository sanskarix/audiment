# Brand Narrative, Tone & Positioning Strategy

This document establishes the canonical brand identity, narrative anchor, copywriting tone, and banned jargon list for **Audiment**.

---

## 🎯 Narrative Anchor

> **"You can't be everywhere."**

Multi-outlet founders and operations directors cannot physically inspect every branch every day. When operators scale from 5 to 50 locations, operational drift occurs—standards slip, audits are faked over WhatsApp, and failures are hidden until regulatory fines or customer complaints occur.

Audiment solves this pain by giving operators **ground-truth operational accountability**.

---

## 🚫 Banned SaaS Jargon List

Audiment's tone is grounded, practical, operational, and founder-native. Never use abstract enterprise jargon in website copy, UI headings, or product communications.

| ❌ Banned Abstract Term | ✅ Approved Founder-Native Alternative |
| :--- | :--- |
| *Operational clarity / visibility* | "Know what's actually happening at every branch." |
| *Streamline compliance* | "Run audits without faking or delay." |
| *Systemic bottlenecks* | "See which outlets keep failing—and why." |
| *Seamlessly / Empower / Leverage* | Use direct action verbs (build, inspect, track, resolve). |
| *Enterprise control infrastructure* | "Audit management system built for multi-location teams." |
| *Pencil-whipping (as main hero title)* | Use as secondary narrative; primary positioning remains audit management. |
