## How do IFM teams standardise inspections across diverse client properties?

> **IFM teams standardise inspections across properties by deploying modular digital checklist templates that separate universal core standards (such as fire exits and washroom hygiene) from asset-specific equipment modules (such as chillers and DG sets).**

Standardising audits across varied portfolios (such as Grade-A tech parks, hospitals, shopping malls, and residential communities) follows a three-tier model:

1. **Universal statutory & life-safety tier:** Applied identically across every site. Inspects fire extinguisher charges, emergency exit clearways, and primary electrical panel conditions.
2. **Asset-specific engineering tier:** Dynamically assigned based on each building's asset register. Technicians scan equipment tags on chillers, diesel generator sets, sewage treatment plants (STP), and water filtration units to trigger specific inspection forms.
3. **Tenant-facing soft services tier:** Evaluates janitorial standards, visual cleanliness, lobby fragrance, and waste segregation using photographic benchmark guides embedded directly inside the checklist.

## What metrics should facility operations directors monitor across properties?

> **Facility operations directors should monitor four core cross-property metrics: Scheduled Round Adherence Rate, First-Time Audit Pass Percentage, Mean Time to Rectify (MTTR) Maintenance Tickets, and Client Audit Satisfaction Score.**

Reviewing enterprise dashboards enables facility executives to benchmark performance across client contracts:
- **Round Adherence Rate (%):** Percentage of planned daily security, janitorial, and engineering rounds executed within their designated time windows.
- **Maintenance MTTR:** Average hours elapsed between flagging an asset defect (such as an elevator breakdown or AHU belt slip) and verified technician sign-off.
- **SLA Risk Index:** Highlights client accounts where audit completion or resolution speed falls below contractual thresholds, enabling proactive leadership intervention before monthly billing reviews.
- **First-Time Fix Ratio (%):** Percentage of mechanical and plumbing defects resolved permanently on the first dispatch without secondary rework.
