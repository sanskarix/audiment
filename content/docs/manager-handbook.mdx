# Manager Operational Handbook

As a **Branch Manager** or **Area Supervisor** (`/dashboard/manager`), your responsibility is maintaining brand excellence and regulatory compliance across your assigned outlets.

Audiment provides a focused workspace showing only your branches, auditors, and action items.

## 1. Manager Overview Dashboard (`/dashboard/manager`)

When you log in, your homepage highlights immediate action items:

- **Assigned Locations & Active Auditors:** Total counts of branches and field personnel under your supervision.
- **Task Priority Trays:**
  - **Unassigned Audits:** Audits published for your locations that have not yet been assigned to a field inspector.
  - **Near Deadlines:** Audits with approaching deadlines that require immediate completion.
- **Recent Audit Score Trend:** An interactive area chart illustrating your locations' compliance trajectory over recent weeks.
- **Inline CAPA Quick-Resolution:** Open corrective actions can be reviewed and resolved directly from the homepage without changing tabs.

---

## 2. Managing & Delegating Audits (`/dashboard/manager/audits`)

When headquarters publishes an audit for your outlet, it appears in your audits queue.

### Single Audit Delegation
1. Open the **Audits** queue.
2. Select an audit in `Scheduled` or `Pending` status.
3. Click the auditor assignment dropdown.
4. Select an active auditor from your roster. The audit status immediately updates to `Assigned`, and the auditor receives an instant notification.

### Bulk Audit Assignment
When handling recurring audits or multiple branches:
1. Use the checkboxes on the left of the audit list to select multiple audits.
2. Click **Bulk Assign**.
3. Choose the target auditor and confirm. All selected audits are assigned in a single action.

---

## 3. Resolving Corrective Actions (`/dashboard/manager/corrective-actions`)

When an auditor discovers a critical failure during an inspection, an automated CAPA ticket is assigned to you with a **strict 48-hour SLA deadline**.

<CapaTicketStepsIllustration />

### Resolution Evidence Requirements
- **Camera Capture:** On mobile devices, clicking the camera icon launches your device's camera directly. Snap a clear photo of the corrected area (e.g., cleared fire exit, sanitized food prep surface, repaired cooler seal).
- **Audit Approval:** Once submitted, your resolution status changes to `Completed` and is submitted to headquarters for final admin sign-off.

---

## 4. Visual Calendar (`/dashboard/manager/calendar`)

Use the monthly calendar view to plan ahead:
- View upcoming non-surprise audits scheduled across the month.
- Monitor execution deadlines to ensure field auditors are dispatched on time.
- Spot days with multiple overlapping inspections to balance auditor workloads.

---

## 5. Reviewing Detailed Audit Reports (`/dashboard/manager/reports/[id]`)

Review full inspection breakdowns for any completed audit:
- Check overall score percentage and grade badge (Excellent, Good, Needs Improvement, or Critical Fail).
- Inspect high-resolution photo evidence attached to individual questions.
- Download verified executive PDF copies for store records or health inspector reviews using the **Export PDF** button.
