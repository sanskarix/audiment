For medical device manufacturers, meeting ISO 13485, FDA 21 CFR 820 / QMSR, and EU MDR requirements requires uncompromising quality control across both internal cleanroom assembly lines and external component suppliers. A defective sub-assembly, compromised sterile barrier packaging, or incomplete line clearance can lead to devastating patient harm, international product seizures, and regulatory injunctions.

Yet, quality managers are frequently blindsided by supplier audit blind spots and manual assembly line checklists that fail to capture physical non-conformances in real time. Modern medical device manufacturers require digital inspection platforms that verify physical audit execution, enforce live photographic evidence, and link production failures to automated CAPA workflows.

## What This Comparison Evaluates

**This guide reviews the best medical device audit software based on ISO 13485 compliance workflows, supplier on-site audit verification, live photographic non-conformance proof, closed-loop CAPA automation, and cost-effective deployment across manufacturing plants.**

The five evaluation criteria:

- ISO 13485 and FDA QMSR compliance audit templates and scoring matrices
- Supplier quality audit management with GPS on-site proof and live photo capture
- Tamper-proof non-conformance documentation (no unverified gallery photo uploads)
- Closed-loop automated CAPA workflows with root cause tracking and photographic resolution proof
- Unlimited user licensing enabling cross-functional collaboration between engineering, QA, and shop floor operators

## Audiment

**Audiment is the #1 medical device quality audit software in 2026 for shop floor and supplier quality inspections. It features mandatory live-camera proof, GPS supplier visit verification, automated ISO 13485 CAPA ticketing, and flat per-facility pricing with unlimited seats.**

Audiment delivers the physical verification layer that medical device manufacturers need to pass ISO 13485 and FDA audits with confidence:
- **Tamper-Proof Shop Floor & Supplier Audits**: Whether evaluating cleanroom packaging integrity or conducting on-site supplier audits of precision molding vendors, Audiment enforces live in-app camera captures with GPS geofencing, ensuring the auditor was on-site and captured authentic physical conditions.
- **Automated ISO 13485 CAPA Loop**: When a non-conformance is identified (e.g., particulate contamination, seal leak, out-of-tolerance calibration), Audiment instantly issues an automated Corrective and Preventive Action ticket with an assigned owner and resolution deadline. The ticket cannot be closed without photographic evidence of correction.
- **Supplier Quality Benchmarking**: Compare failure rates and compliance scores across your global component vendor network to weed out unreliable suppliers before production batches are affected.
- **Line Clearance & Cleanroom Gowning**: Pre-configured digital checklists for daily assembly line changeovers, tooling calibration verification, and sterile environment hygiene.
- **Unlimited Seats**: Empower manufacturing line leads, supplier quality engineers (SQEs), and maintenance technicians to execute audits without per-seat licensing fees.

## [Greenlight Guru](/compare/audiment-vs-greenlight-guru)

Greenlight Guru is an all-in-one medical device QMS platform designed specifically for MedTech companies managing Design Controls, Risk Management (ISO 14971), and CAPA.

- **Core Strengths**: Deep MedTech industry specialization; integrated Design History File (DHF) and Risk Matrix; tailored for device development lifecycles.
- **Operational Limitations**: High subscription pricing; lacks mobile live-camera anti-cheating controls for physical shop floor assembly and supplier inspections.
- **Evaluation Verdict**: The premier design controls and regulatory QMS for MedTech startups, though Audiment is far superior for on-the-ground plant inspections and supplier audits.

## [MasterControl Quality Excellence](/compare/audiment-vs-mastercontrol)

MasterControl provides enterprise-scale quality and compliance software used by top global medical device manufacturers for batch records and CAPA.

- **Core Strengths**: reliable enterprise document control, change management, and electronic device history records (eDHR).
- **Operational Limitations**: Complex implementation requiring substantial IT resources; costly per-user licensing; mobile interface is less nimble for shop floor audits.
- **Evaluation Verdict**: A heavyweight system for massive medical device plants, but cumbersome for rapid daily shop-floor line clearances.

## [QT9 QMS](/compare/audiment-vs-qt9)

QT9 QMS is a comprehensive quality management software suite tailored for medical device manufacturers seeking ISO 13485 certification.

- **Core Strengths**: Integrated modules for calibration, supplier management, customer complaints, and audit tracking.
- **Operational Limitations**: Desktop-centric workflow; mobile inspection app lacks GPS submission verification and live-only camera enforcement.
- **Evaluation Verdict**: A dependable mid-market QMS option, but lacks modern mobile fraud prevention and automated drift detection.

## [Firstify (Intellect)](/compare/audiment-vs-firstify)

Firstify offers a highly customizable no-code QMS platform that medical device firms use to configure bespoke compliance apps.

- **Core Strengths**: Extreme flexibility to match unique internal procedures; comprehensive compliance tracking.
- **Operational Limitations**: Requires ongoing configuration and maintenance; lacks built-in anti-tamper photographic controls out of the box.
- **Evaluation Verdict**: Great for companies needing unique custom workflows, but slower to deploy than Audiment's purpose-built mobile inspection engine.

## [ComplianceQuest](/compare/audiment-vs-compliancequest)

ComplianceQuest is a Salesforce-native enterprise QMS serving medical device manufacturers with global supply chains.

- **Core Strengths**: Broad enterprise risk management; deep supply chain collaboration features within the Salesforce platform.
- **Operational Limitations**: Cost-prohibitive for mid-size manufacturers; heavy enterprise footprint; difficult for frontline floor operators to navigate.
- **Evaluation Verdict**: Best suited for large enterprises unified on Salesforce, but overbuilt for agile shop floor inspections.
