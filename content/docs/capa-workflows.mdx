# Automated Corrective Action (CAPA) Workflows

Audits without enforcement are just paperwork. When an auditor records a major violation—such as improper refrigeration temperatures, blocked emergency exits, or missing food labels—simply logging the problem is not enough.

Audiment features an automated **CAPA (Corrective and Preventive Action)** engine that bridges the gap between inspection findings and physical operational resolution.

## 1. Automatic Ticket Generation

Whenever an auditor answers "No" to a critical Yes/No question, or awards a low score on a critical rating question:
- Audiment's scoring engine marks the response as a **Critical Failure**.
- A new CAPA record is instantly generated in the organization's database.
- The CAPA inherits the auditor's field notes, question description, and location metadata.
- A **48-hour deadline countdown** starts immediately.
- The designated Branch Manager receives an automated high-priority alert.

---

## 2. Manager Resolution Workflow

Branch Managers do not need desktop computers to handle CAPA tasks. They can review and rectify issues directly from their smartphone dashboard:

<CapaResolutionModalIllustration />

### Resolution Requirements
1. **Mandatory Resolution Notes:** The manager must explain what corrective action was taken and what preventative measures were established.
2. **Mandatory Photographic Proof:** Words alone cannot close a CAPA. The manager must use the in-app camera or file picker to upload verified photos showing the violation has been fixed.
3. **Audit Trail Timestamp:** The resolution is stamped with the exact time of completion and manager ID.

---

## 3. Executive Review & Governance

Once marked completed by a manager, Organization Admins review the resolution evidence:

- **Accept / Resolved:** The admin verifies the photographic proof and marks the CAPA as officially `resolved`.
- **Reject / Re-open:** If the photo is unclear, incomplete, or the corrective action is deemed insufficient, the admin rejects the submission, sending the item back to `in_progress` with feedback notes.
- **Overdue Tracking:** If a CAPA exceeds 48 hours without resolution, it transitions to `overdue` status, triggering escalated notifications to headquarters and docking the location's compliance health score.
