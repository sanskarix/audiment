# Auditor Operational Handbook

The **Auditor Mobile Interface** (`/dashboard/auditor`) is optimized for touchscreen smartphones, enabling field staff to conduct step-by-step inspections on-site.

---

## 📱 Conducting an Inspection

### 1. Starting an Audit
* Log into your auditor account on your mobile browser.
* Select an assigned audit from the **Assigned Audits** list.

### 2. Step-by-Step Question Interface
* Read the question criteria.
* Select **Yes / No** or choose a numeric rating from **1 to 10**.
* If a camera icon displays `Photo Required`, tap **Take Photo** to snap live evidence.
* Optionally type notes explaining specific site observations.
* Tap **Next Question**.

### 3. Draft Auto-Save & Abort Guardrails
* **Progress Auto-Save:** You can exit an active audit at any time; your answers are saved automatically and can be resumed later.
* **Abort Guardrail:** If you tap the back button accidentally, a confirmation modal prevents data loss.

### 4. Flash Verification (Flashmob)
* If your profile has Flashmob access, tap **Flash Verification**.
* Select target location.
* Press **Record** to take a 20-second live camera sweep of your surroundings.
* Align your face in the circular **Face Guide** and capture a selfie snapshot.
* Tap **Submit**.
