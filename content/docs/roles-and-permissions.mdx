# Roles & Access Control Architecture

Audiment enforces strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) to ensure that every team member sees exactly what they need—and nothing they shouldn't.

Whether you run 3 company-owned flagship stores or a network of 200+ franchise outlets, permissions are strictly partitioned across three primary roles.

## Role Deep-Dives

### 1. The Admin Role
The Admin account represents company headquarters, franchise directors, or quality assurance (QA) leadership.

- **Primary URL:** `/dashboard/admin`
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Standardize operational standards across all brand locations.
  - Create and manage the master directory of outlets and branches.
  - Provision team member accounts (assigning Managers to specific branches and pairing Auditors).
  - Review network-wide compliance analytics, spot underperforming branches, and receive real-time trend warnings.
  - Final sign-off on resolved high-severity Corrective Actions.

### 2. The Branch Manager Role
The Manager account is tailored for General Managers, Area Managers, or Kitchen Supervisors overseeing one or several branches.

- **Primary URL:** `/dashboard/manager`
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Receive automated audit assignments for their designated locations.
  - Delegate inspection queues to field auditors.
  - Respond immediately to critical audit failures and overdue warnings.
  - Resolve open CAPA tickets within the 48-hour SLA window by uploading photographic proof of resolution and descriptive corrective actions.
  - Track branch score trajectories and staff performance through the monthly audit calendar.

### 3. The Field Auditor Role
The Auditor account is built exclusively for field quality inspectors, compliance officers, and kitchen hygiene evaluators operating on mobile phones.

- **Primary URL:** `/dashboard/auditor`
- **Key Responsibilities:**
  - Access their daily queue of pending inspections.
  - Complete checklist criteria step-by-step with zero distractions.
  - Capture live, un-manipulated photographic evidence using the browser-based camera viewfinder.
  - Confirm physical presence on-site via enforced high-accuracy GPS verification.
  - Execute stealth Flash Verification video presence sweeps when dispatched.

## Multi-Tenant Security & Data Isolation

Audiment guarantees absolute data privacy and isolation between different client organizations:

1. **Organization Boundary:** Every data entity—blueprints, questions, responses, photos, and compliance scores—is stamped with an immutable organization identifier.
2. **Zero Cross-Org Leakage:** An auditor or manager in Brand A cannot view, query, or interact with records belonging to Brand B.
3. **Session Verification:** Every API call and page load verifies role claims server-side before serving inspection data or audit reports.
