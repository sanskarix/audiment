# Surprise Audits & Recurring Scheduling Engine

In multi-location operations, the element of timing dictates the reliability of inspection data. Audiment provides two complementary scheduling engines:

1. **Surprise Audits:** Unannounced, short-deadline inspections designed to capture unvarnished reality.
2. **Recurring Audits:** Fully automated schedules (daily, weekly, monthly) that maintain continuous operational discipline.

---

## 1. Surprise Audits Engine

When store staff know an inspector arrives every Tuesday at 10:00 AM, they spend Monday night deep-cleaning and correcting deficiencies. The audit score looks pristine, but operations return to normal the moment the inspector departs.

Audiment's **Surprise Audit** capability eliminates this artificial "prep bias."

<SurpriseAuditIllustration />

### Key Surprise Audit Mechanics
- **Hidden from Branch Views:** Surprise audits do not appear on manager calendar views or upcoming audit queues ahead of time.
- **Short Execution Windows:** Tight deadlines prevent staff from tipping off neighboring branches.
- **Dedicated Alert Channels:** When published, managers receive an unannounced alert notification, while auditors receive real-time dispatch directions.

---

## 2. Automated Recurring Schedules

For standard operational routines—such as daily kitchen opening checklists, weekly pest control logs, or monthly brand merchandising checks—Audiment automates recurring generation.

<RecurringScheduleIllustration />

### Supported Frequencies & Rules
- **Daily Audits:** Automatically publishes every calendar day at the location's designated start time.
- **Weekly Audits:** Configured for specific days of the week (e.g., Every Monday or Every Thursday).
- **Monthly Audits:** Configured for specific days of the month (e.g., 1st or 15th of every month).
- **Duration Window Inheritance:** The time delta between the scheduled start time and deadline (e.g., 3 hours) is preserved across all future recurring instances.
- **Location-Specific Default Times:** If a branch opens at 07:00 AM instead of the organization default of 09:00 AM, the scheduler automatically respects the branch-level operating hours.

---

## 3. Automated Missed Audit Handling

If an auditor fails to complete an inspection before its deadline:
- The system automatically transitions the audit status to **Missed**.
- Both the branch manager and headquarters receive an immediate missed audit alert.
- The missed event is recorded in the branch's historical reliability metrics, preventing "forgotten" audits from slipping through the cracks.
