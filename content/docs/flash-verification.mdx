# Flash Verification (Flashmob)

**Flash Verification** (known internally as *Flashmob*) is Audiment's covert, rapid video-based presence verification engine. It was developed specifically to solve the hardest problem in multi-location audit integrity: **how to verify what an outlet actually looks like right now, without warning.**

---

## Why Standard Checklists Aren't Always Enough

Standard scheduled checklists give branch managers advance warning. While scheduled audits are essential for routine compliance, they suffer from the **"inspection effect"**:
- Staff clean up 30 minutes before the scheduled audit time.
- Temporary fixes hide systemic operational violations.
- Once the auditor leaves, habits return to unmonitored drift.

Flash Verification breaks this cycle by dispatching unannounced, unedited video audit requests that take under 2 minutes to complete.

## Step-by-Step Execution Flow

### Step 1: Permission Gate & Location Selection
- Flashmob capabilities are role-gated. Admins grant permission individually to trusted auditors via the User Management panel (`isFlashmobEnabled = true`).
- The Admin selects a target location and dispatches a time-sensitive flash request.

### Step 2: 20-Second Surroundings Sweep
- Upon opening the request on their mobile phone, the auditor steps onto the floor and initiates the recording.
- The interface opens a continuous 20-second video stream with a live recording counter (`REC 00:20`).
- The auditor performs an unedited 360-degree pan of the dining room, kitchen line, service counter, or back-of-house storage.

### Step 3: Face Guide & Circular Selfie Verification
- Immediately after the 20-second sweep finishes, the camera prompts the auditor to switch to the front camera.
- A circular viewfinder overlay requires the auditor to align their face within the target guide, ensuring that the person capturing the video is the authorized inspector.

### Step 4: Encrypted Direct Streaming & GPS Binding
- The completed video clip and selfie are bundled with high-accuracy GPS coordinates and transmitted directly to encrypted cloud storage.
- Video files are processed for instant streaming playback on the Admin executive dashboard.

## Typical Use Cases for Flashmob Audits

1. **Peak Rush Hour Audits:** See kitchen line order, hygiene, and cleanliness during Friday night dinner peak without disrupting service.
2. **Franchise Brand Compliance:** Verify menu board signage, uniforms, and marketing posters across franchise networks without sending formal warning.
3. **Pest & Cleanliness Spot Checks:** Inspect dumpster pads, grease trap corners, and dry storage areas during closing shifts.
4. **Immediate Incident Verification:** If a customer complaint or safety report is filed, dispatch an auditor within the hour to capture unvarnished proof.
