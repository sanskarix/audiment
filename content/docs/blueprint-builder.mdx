# Blueprint Template Builder & FSSAI Standards

The **Blueprint Builder** is the foundational authoring engine in Audiment. Accessible to Organization Admins, it enables operations heads to translate operational standards, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and hygiene checklists into structured digital inspection blueprints.

## Core Question Properties

Each question in a blueprint can be configured with specific attributes that dictate how field auditors answer and how scores are calculated:

### 1. Question Text
Clear, concise instructions specifying what standard is being evaluated. Keep questions objective and unambiguous (e.g., *"Are raw meats stored separately from ready-to-eat items?"* rather than *"Is the kitchen clean?"*).

### 2. Response Types
- **Yes / No:** Binary evaluation. A "Yes" represents full compliance (1.0), while "No" represents total failure (0.0).
- **Rating Scale (1 to 10):** Numeric evaluation for graduated assessment (e.g., staff uniform cleanliness, customer area ambiance, food presentation). Scores are normalized linearly.

### 3. Severity Weighting
Audiment weights questions based on real-world operational risk:

| Severity Level | Mathematical Weight | Failure Consequence |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Low** | `1x` | Minor aesthetic or administrative deficiency. Reduces overall score slightly. |
| **Medium** | `2x` | Operational drift that requires attention. Moderate score impact. |
| **Critical** | `5x` | **Severe breach.** A failure immediately triggers an automated 48-hour Corrective Action (CAPA) ticket and overrides the audit grade. |

### 4. Mandatory Photo Proof (`requiresPhoto`)
When toggled on, the auditor's mobile interface physically blocks progression until at least one photo is captured through the in-app camera or uploaded. This is recommended for:
- Food contact surface sanitation
- Temperature display gauges
- Fire exit clearance
- Pest control traps and logbooks

## 1-Click FSSAI Food Safety Defaults

For food service, restaurant, and cloud kitchen operators in India, Audiment includes pre-built **FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India)** compliance standards.

Clicking **Load FSSAI Defaults** in the builder automatically populates an exhaustive standard checklist adhering to statutory Schedule 4 requirements, covering:
- **Location and Surroundings:** Clean surroundings, drainage, distance from contamination sources.
- **Layout and Design:** Pest exclusion, cross-contamination barriers, non-absorbent surfaces.
- **Equipment and Utensils:** Food-grade contact surfaces, calibration, temperature monitoring.
- **Personal Hygiene:** Clean chef coats, hairnets, medical fitness records, handwashing stations.
- **Water Quality & Waste:** Potable water testing certifications, segregated waste bins with lids.

## Best Practices for Authoring Blueprints

1. **Keep Checklists Focused:** A field inspection should rarely exceed 35–45 questions to prevent auditor fatigue and rushed answers.
2. **Prioritize Critical Failure Points:** Limit Critical severity to true deal-breakers (e.g., cross-contamination, expired permits, severe pest sightings). Marking every question as Critical dilutes manager urgency.
3. **Use Logical Grouping:** Order questions in the natural physical path an auditor walks through the facility (Receiving Bay → Dry Storage → Walk-in Coolers → Prep Line → Cooking Line → Service Counter).
