## 2. Changes by Page

### Page 1: Restaurant Audit Software (`/solutions/restaurant-audit-software`)

#### Objective & Framing
Positioned as the **broad, quality-and-compliance management platform** for multi-unit restaurant groups, dining concepts, and cloud/commissary kitchens.

#### Key Content Changes
1. **Direct Upfront Buyer Answer (Hero & Overview):**
   - Directly addresses how multi-location restaurant chains maintain consistency across opening, line prep, food safety, and closing shifts.
   - Clarifies that Audiment replaces scattered paper clipboards with digital checklists, mandatory visual evidence capture, and real-time head-office tracking.
2. **Opening, Closing, Cleanliness & Food Safety Standardization:**
   - Detailed breakdown of standardized digital blueprints for cold-chain monitoring (≤ 4°C walk-in/prep tables), hot holding (≥ 60°C), FIFO ingredient labeling, and nightly sanitation procedures.
   - Specific sample checklist entries covering morning setup, food contact surface sanitization, prep line rotation, tamper packaging, and closing security.
3. **Head-Office Visibility & Chronic Failure Detection:**
   - Grounded strictly in real product dashboards: organization compliance percentages, outlet score averages, and color-coded visual performance charts (green ≥ 90%, blue 70%–89%, red < 70%).
   - Highlights the automated **3-consecutive low-score trend alert** that escalates chronic branch failures directly to corporate managers.
4. **Concrete Corrective Action (CAPA) Workflow Example:**
   - Walkthrough of a real kitchen scenario: an auditor discovers an open prep chiller at 9°C (target ≤ 4°C) during an unannounced inspection.
   - The failure triggers an automated task assigned to the branch manager with an enforceable 48-hour SLA.
   - Resolution requires documented actions and fresh photographic evidence of the repaired chiller showing compliant readings before corporate closure.
5. **Dedicated Cloud Kitchen & Commissary Section:**
   - Explicitly details shared-equipment sanitation protocols between virtual brand production shifts.
   - Covers raw allergen segregation, batch hot-holding temperature logging, and tamper-evident packaging checks prior to aggregator delivery dispatch.
6. **Factual Boundary Enforcement:**
   - Removed inaccurate claims regarding offline synchronization (clarified that active network is required for submission and photo uploads).
   - Removed claims regarding duplicate-photo detection and unverified time savings.

### Page 2: QSR Audit Software (`/solutions/qsr-audit-software`)

#### Objective & Framing
Positioned as the **high-velocity, shift-by-shift operational operating system** built specifically for rapid-service, high-transaction fast-food chains scaling from 10 to 30–50 locations.

#### Key Content Changes
1. **The 30–50 Outlet Operational Challenge (Hero & Overview):**
   - Directly targets the critical failure point when QSR chains scale past 20–30 locations: regional coaches become overwhelmed, paper checklists get pencil-whipped, and brand standards diverge across shifts.
   - Explains how digital audits enforce repeatable consistency across morning prep, peak meal rushes, and evening closings.
2. **Shift-by-Shift Standardized Execution:**
   - Structured checklists for line calibrations (fryer oil temperatures at 175°C, holding cabinets at ≥ 60°C, sanitizer PPM concentrations, drive-thru queue clearing).
   - Enforces digital accountability during shift transitions to prevent opening teams from inheriting dirty prep lines from overnight crews.
3. **Regional Manager & Head-Office Oversight:**
   - Details regional coach territory dashboards: tracking assigned store score trajectories, unassigned audits, and open corrective actions.
   - Highlights how multi-location operators use organization-wide compliance matrices and automated 3-strike low-score alerts to isolate operational drift in specific territories.
4. **Practical 4-Phase Rollout Framework:**
   - **Phase 1: Core Blueprint Standardization** (head-office standardization of 2–3 core checklists).
   - **Phase 2: Pilot Cluster Deployment** (5–10 representative high-volume company stores to calibrate timing and question clarity).
   - **Phase 3: Territory & Manager Node Mapping** (configuring location nodes, assigning store managers, and grouping regional area coaches).
   - **Phase 4: Network Shift Activation & Trend Monitoring** (activating recurring shift schedules and tracking corporate compliance dashboards).
5. **Pillar Blog Integration:**
   - Updated the primary educational link directly to `/blog/how-restaurant-chains-maintain-consistency-across-50-locations` ("How Restaurant Chains Maintain Consistency Across 50 Locations").

## 7. Remaining Factual Blockers / Owner Clarifications

The following boundaries were respected and require product team action before marketing claims can expand:
1. **Offline Mode:** The platform requires an active network connection for uploading photos and submitting audits. Offline mode is not claimed.
2. **Brand-Level Multi-Tenant Permissions:** Audiment uses an Organization -> Location hierarchy. Multi-brand permission silos within a single organization are not currently supported natively in code and were not promised.
3. **Fixed Onboarding Promises:** No arbitrary "14-day onboarding" or guaranteed regulatory compliance (e.g. FSSAI clearance) claims were made.
