# Admin Operational Handbook

The **Admin Dashboard** (`/dashboard/admin`) is the headquarters control room for your organization. As an Admin, you configure the master rules, manage outlets, invite personnel, and review network compliance.

---

## Quick Setup: Launching Your Organization in 4 Steps

<AdminOnboardingIllustration />

---

## 1. Managing Locations (`/dashboard/admin/locations`)

Locations represent your physical business units (restaurants, retail stores, warehouses, or clinics).

### Adding a New Location
1. Navigate to **Locations** in the admin sidebar.
2. Click **Add Location**.
3. Fill in the required fields:
   - **Location Name:** Clear branch identifier (e.g., *"Indiranagar Flagship - Store #04"*).
   - **Address & City:** Physical location details.
   - **Default Operating Hours:** Set `defaultStartTime` and `defaultEndTime`. When audits are scheduled for this outlet, start and deadline times auto-populate to match these hours.
   - **Assigned Managers:** Multi-select one or more Managers responsible for supervising this branch.
4. Click **Save Location**.

> **Note on Cascading Updates:** If you rename a location later, Audiment automatically updates all historical and active audits, CAPA records, and reports associated with that branch.

---

## 2. Managing Users & Staff (`/dashboard/admin/users`)

Admins manage user accounts for both Managers and Field Auditors.

### Inviting a New Team Member
1. Navigate to **Users** in the sidebar.
2. Click **New User**.
3. Select the role:
   - **Manager:** Has authority over designated locations, assigns audits, and resolves CAPAs.
   - **Auditor:** Field inspector conducting mobile checklist audits.
4. Provide the user's name, email, and a temporary password.
5. **Auditor-Specific Settings:**
   - **Managing Manager:** Assign which Manager oversees this auditor's tasks.
   - **Flashmob Access Toggle (`isFlashmobEnabled`):** Enable or disable whether this auditor can receive stealth video presence audit requests.
6. Click **Create User**.

### Editing & Managing Existing Users
- **Password Resets:** Click the pencil icon on any user row and select **Send Password Reset Email** to send a secure recovery link.
- **Deactivate User:** Toggle the active switch to immediately disable access without losing historical audit logs.

---

## 3. Creating Master Blueprints (`/dashboard/admin/templates`)

Blueprints are the master templates used to conduct audits.

1. Navigate to **Templates** > **New Template**.
2. Enter the template title, category (e.g., *Hygiene*, *Safety*, *Merchandising*), and brief instructions.
3. Click **Add Question** or **Load FSSAI Defaults** for pre-configured food safety questions.
4. Configure severity weights (`Low`, `Medium`, `Critical`) and toggle **Requires Photo** for essential visual criteria.
5. Save the blueprint to make it available for audit scheduling.

---

## 4. Scheduling & Dispatching Audits (`/dashboard/admin/audits`)

1. Click **New Audit**.
2. Select the **Template**, **Target Location**, and **Assigned Manager**.
3. Choose the **Scheduled Date** and **Deadline**.
4. Configure recurrence:
   - **One-time:** Single inspection.
   - **Daily / Weekly / Monthly:** Automatic recurring generation.
5. **Surprise Audit Toggle:** Turn on to make the inspection unannounced (hidden from the manager's upcoming calendar).
6. Click **Publish Audit**.

---

## 5. Overseeing Corrective Actions (`/dashboard/admin/corrective-actions`)

1. Review the org-wide CAPA queue sorted by urgency (Closest Deadline → Critical Severity → Status).
2. Filter by status: `Open`, `In Progress`, `Resolved`, or `Overdue`.
3. When a manager completes a CAPA with notes and photo evidence, click **Review**:
   - Tap the resolution photo thumbnail to view full-screen high-res proof.
   - Click **Accept & Resolve** to formally close the violation.
   - Click **Reject** to send it back to the manager if evidence is unsatisfactory.
