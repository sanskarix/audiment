When enterprise procurement teams and operations directors review SaaS proposals for audit management software, the headline price on the proposal sheet frequently looks straightforward: *$19 per user per month* or *₹2,500 per location per quarter*. However, enterprise buyers who sign standard vendor agreements without interrogating contractual fine print often experience cost inflation of 40% to 120% by month six.

Unexpected line items, such as user role upgrade fees, photo storage overage penalties, custom PDF template charges, and compulsory implementation retainers,turn an apparently affordable operational tool into a contentious budget overrun.

## How does per-user pricing penalize multi-location businesses?

> **Per-user pricing penalizes multi-location businesses because store networks naturally scale by locations and frontline staff, forcing companies to either pay exorbitant seat fees for every shift supervisor or restrict logins, creating dangerous operational blind spots.**

The structural flaw in per-user software models across retail and restaurant chains stems from workforce realities:

```
The Per-User Licensing Dilemma:

40 Stores x 2 Shift Supervisors + 1 Store Manager = 120 Frontline Users
                                   |
         +-------------------------+-------------------------+
         |                                                   |
Option A: Purchase 120 User Licenses                 Option B: Share 1 Login Per Store
Cost: 120 x $29/mo = $41,760/Year                   Cost: 40 x $29/mo = $13,920/Year
Result: Software budget blown out by 300%.          Result: Credential sharing; zero
                                                    user accountability; breaks audit trail.
```

In contrast, **flat per-location pricing** allows unlimited users per store. Every shift supervisor, assistant manager, maintenance technician, and regional director can have their own named account without adding incremental software costs.

## What contractual traps hide in cloud photo storage terms?

> **Photo storage traps hide in restrictive monthly gigabyte allowances and high overage penalties. Because proof-based audits mandate 5 to 10 high-resolution photos per inspection, multi-outlet networks quickly breach standard storage tiers within 90 days.**

| Inspection Frequency | Photos Per Month (40 Outlets) | Storage Volume (Monthly) | Standard Vendor Storage Cap | Typical Overage Penalty |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Daily Opening Checks** | 3,600 Photos | ~7.2 GB | 10 GB Total | $0.20 per GB / Month |
| **Weekly Manager Audits**| 4,800 Photos | ~9.6 GB | Exceeded in Month 2 | Escalating cost overage |
| **Maintenance CAPA Proof**| 2,400 Photos | ~4.8 GB | Exceeded in Month 2 | Storage locked unless paid |
| **Total Evidence Volume**| **10,800 Photos/Mo** | **~21.6 GB / Month** | **Breached rapidly** | **Compounding storage debt** |

Before signing any contract, require the vendor to provide:
1. **Unlimited photo evidence storage** included in the base platform fee.
2. **Automated client-side image compression** that preserves metadata (UTC time, GPS, resolution) while reducing raw image file size by 70%.
3. **Guaranteed multi-year retention** (minimum 3 to 7 years) without secondary archive retrieval fees.

## What should you check regarding implementation and onboarding fees?

> **Check whether implementation fees are compulsory or optional, what exact deliverables are included, whether template digitisation is bundled or billed hourly, and whether on-site store staff training requires expensive travel retainers.**

Vendor implementation proposals often bury significant service charges under vague headings:

```
Procurement Audit Checklist for Implementation Proposals:

[ ] Template Digitisation:
    Are checklist conversions included, or billed at ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 per template?
    Ensure vendor contractually guarantees conversion of your first 5 master checklists.

[ ] Store Geofence & Asset Configuration:
    Does the vendor configure your 40 store coordinates, or is setup left to your team?

[ ] Staff Training Sessions:
    How many live virtual webinars are included? Are recorded training videos provided?

[ ] Dedicated Account Management:
    Is phone and WhatsApp support included, or restricted to a slow email ticketing queue
    unless you purchase an expensive "Enterprise Premium Support" tier?
```

## What procurement questions expose hidden costs before contract signing?

> **Procurement teams should ask five specific questions: Are store manager accounts charged separately? What are the exact photo storage limits? Are PDF report customizations free? What are the API integration fees? What are the contract exit costs?**

### The 5 Essential Vendor Procurement Questions:

1. *"If our store count remains at 40, but we add 80 assistant managers to the platform, will our monthly invoice increase by even one rupee?"*
2. *"If our inspectors upload 25,000 photos next month, will any storage overage or bandwidth surcharge appear on our bill?"*
3. *"Is there any charge for exporting our complete historical audit data, photos, and CAPA logs into CSV and zip archives if we terminate the contract?"*
4. *"Can we modify, create, and re-weight our own checklist templates without paying professional service fees to your consulting team?"*
5. *"Does your platform charge separately for sending automated WhatsApp and SMS incident alerts to our maintenance engineers?"*

A transparent software partner answers these questions clearly in writing within the Master Services Agreement (MSA).
