# Photo Proof & Geolocation Enforcement

Traditional checklist applications allow auditors to fill out forms from their living room, coffee shop, or car outside the premises. In high-stakes multi-location operations, this "pencil-whipping" blinds leadership to real conditions.

Audiment enforces **hardware-level verification** at two checkpoints:
1. **Live In-App Photo Capture** (Visual Ground Truth)
2. **GPS Geolocation Locking** (Physical Presence Verification)

---

## 1. Live In-App Camera Viewfinder

When an audit question specifies `requiresPhoto`, field auditors cannot bypass it with a text note or synthetic checkmark.

<CameraCaptureIllustration />

### Key Camera Capabilities
- **Native Device Stream:** Directly taps into the smartphone or tablet camera hardware via modern browser APIs.
- **Camera Flipping:** Seamlessly switch between the rear environment camera (for equipment, shelves, food prep areas) and front camera (for selfie presence checks).
- **Selfie Mirror Correction:** Front-camera captures are horizontally flipped automatically to ensure text on uniforms, badges, or signage reads legibly.
- **Multi-Photo Evidence:** Auditors can attach multiple photographs to a single question when comprehensive visual documentation is required.
- **Cloud Upload Pipeline:** Captured photos are streamed securely to encrypted cloud media storage without caching sensitive files on unauthorized personal device folders.

---

## 2. GPS Geolocation Enforcement

Even with live photos, how can leadership be certain the auditor didn't take pictures yesterday or receive them via chat?

Audiment binds every audit submission to real-time physical GPS coordinates.

<GeoVerificationIllustration />

### Geolocation Safety Rules
- **Enforced at Final Submission:** The auditor's browser must supply high-accuracy geolocation (`enableHighAccuracy: true`).
- **Submission Blocked on Failure:** If location permissions are blocked, or the device GPS is disabled, **the audit CANNOT be finalized**. A prompt guides the auditor to re-enable location services.
- **Audit Ledger Metadata:** Once approved, exact coordinates and capture timestamps are permanently written into the immutable audit record and rendered on executive PDF reports.

---

## Preventing Operational Drift with Visual Truth

When field auditors know that:
1. Photos are required on critical health items, and
2. Their physical presence is confirmed by GPS coordinates,

Compliance accuracy increases dramatically. Operational drift is caught in days rather than waiting for quarterly executive branch tours.
