# Reporting, Analytics & PDF Export Engine

Raw audit data is only valuable if operations leaders can spot patterns, compare branches, and take decisive action. Audiment pairs real-time compliance dashboards with a client-side, high-fidelity PDF export engine.

---

## 1. Executive Compliance Dashboards

Audiment's analytics dashboards aggregate performance across all active locations, highlighting operational drift before it impacts customers or regulators.

<ComplianceDashboardIllustration />

### Dashboard Capabilities
- **Real-Time Score Aggregation:** Average scores recalculate instantaneously as field auditors submit inspections.
- **Color-Coded Health Bands:**
  - **High Compliance (≥ 90%):** Pristine compliance and brand consistency.
  - **Operational Drift (70% – 89%):** Satisfactory performance with minor operational drift.
  - **Critical Risk (< 70%):** High-risk branch requiring immediate operational review.
- **Trend Detection Alerts:** If an outlet scores below threshold on 3 or more consecutive inspections, Audiment triggers a high-visibility warning banner across Admin and Manager homepages.

---

## 2. High-Fidelity PDF Export Engine

For board meetings, franchise owner reviews, health inspector submissions, and insurance audits, digital screenshots are inadequate. Audiment features a built-in client-side PDF export pipeline that renders clean, branded audit documentation.

<PdfReportIllustration />

### PDF Export Specifications
- **Multi-Page Layout:** Renders crisp multi-page A4 documents with running headers and pagination.
- **Embedded Evidence Photos:** High-resolution evidence photos are scaled, framed, and embedded directly beside their corresponding checklist questions.
- **Audit Metadata Stamping:** Includes verified auditor name, branch address, GPS coordinates, date, and final scoring grade.
- **Consistent Naming Scheme:** Exports adhere to standardized naming: `{Location}-{Template}-{ddMMMyyy}.pdf` for effortless archiving in Google Drive, Dropbox, or company servers.

---

## 3. CSV Data Export

Admins can also export all raw historical audit data across their entire organization to CSV directly from Settings:
- Includes audit titles, outlet names, assigned managers, field inspectors, completion dates, final scores, and completion statuses.
- Formatted for immediate import into Business Intelligence (BI) platforms such as Tableau, PowerBI, or Google Sheets.
