## 3. How to Use This KB to Remodel Content for Other Platforms (SEO Workflow)

1. **Pick a Buyer-Priority Topic:** Start with `P1` (`04`, `05`) or `P2` (`06`, `07`, `02`, `03`, `09`) depending on the target audience.
2. **Pull Real Substance (No Generic Intro Fluff):** Grab concrete thresholds (`1°C–4°C`, `-18°C`, `12h/24h/48h CAPA`, `20s Flash Verification`, `critical_fail` scoring override), statutory tables (FSSAI Form IX, IS 10500, FoSTaC), or competitor cost math (`$24–$29/user/mo` vs flat unlimited pricing).
3. **Attach Exact Citations:** Every claim/table in the KB cites the canonical `https://audiment.in/blog/<slug>` post and external standard (`ISO 19011:2018`, `FSSAI Schedule 4`, etc.). Link 1–3 relevant `/blog/...` or `/solutions/...` or `/compare/...` URLs from [13-audiment-messaging-and-product-canonical.md](file:///Users/sanskar/Documents/Asellus/Audiment/docs/knowledge-base/13-audiment-messaging-and-product-canonical.md) depending on platform link norms.
4. **Check Keywords & AEO Questions:** Pull the exact search phrases and question headers from [14-seo-keywords-and-aeo-questions-bank.md](file:///Users/sanskar/Documents/Asellus/Audiment/docs/knowledge-base/14-seo-keywords-and-aeo-questions-bank.md) for titles, H2s, and Quora/Reddit queries.
5. **Apply Founder-Native Voice:** Run a final pass against the banned jargon table in [13-audiment-messaging-and-product-canonical.md](file:///Users/sanskar/Documents/Asellus/Audiment/docs/knowledge-base/13-audiment-messaging-and-product-canonical.md) (cut *streamline, leverage, empower, seamlessly, operational clarity*).
