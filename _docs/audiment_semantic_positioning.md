### Foundational Insight
Visibility means you can see what was reported.  
Verification means you can trust what was reported.

Most competitors optimize for:
- Visibility
- Documentation
- Reporting
- Task tracking

Audiment optimizes for:
- Proof
- Verification
- Evidence integrity
- Operational trust
- Anti-pencil-whipping
- Reliable operational visibility across locations
- Getting trends and signals from auditing data over time

That is the category wedge.

## 4. Core Narrative

### Core Positioning Line

- Audits can be faked

- Checklists get filled from memory
- Managers cannot physically verify every outlet

Turn audits into evidence-backed operational truth.
