## What metrics demonstrate internal audit efficiency across multi-unit operations?

> **Internal audit efficiency is measured using four core operational metrics: Audit Cycle Velocity, Audit Plan Completion Rate, CAPA Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR), and Repeat Finding Ratio across locations.**

Audit leadership tracks department performance through four indicators:
- **Audit Cycle Velocity (Days):** Time elapsed between concluding on-site fieldwork and releasing the finalized, signed executive audit report. Modern teams achieve this in under 24 hours.
- **Audit Plan Adherence (%):** Percentage of planned quarterly and annual facility audits successfully executed within their scheduled target windows.
- **CAPA MTTR (Hours):** Average hours taken by frontline operations teams to physically remediate and verify audit non-conformances.
- **Repeat Non-Conformance Density (%):** Frequency with which identical operational defects reappear across consecutive audit cycles at the same facility.
