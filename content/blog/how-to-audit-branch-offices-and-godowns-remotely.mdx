## What is a remote branch and godown audit?

> **A remote branch and godown audit is an operational verification model where local station masters complete digital inspection checklists using locked-down mobile devices. The software captures GPS-verified coordinates, prevents gallery uploads by requiring real-time photos, and routes failure reports directly to central compliance teams without requiring on-site auditor travel.**

```

[Audiment Remote Model]

Result: Daily/Weekly inspection cadence, zero travel expense, instant regional dashboard visibility
```

## How do you prevent local staff from faking remote godown audits?

> **Preventing local staff from manipulating remote audits requires three technical safeguards: strict device geolocation boundaries that reject submissions outside the facility perimeter, real-time camera capture that disables gallery uploads, and mandatory secondary sign-off with timestamped visual proof before resolving any non-compliance issue.**

When station managers or branch in-charges self-audit their premises, paper forms and WhatsApp messages invite rubber-stamping. Operators upload recycled images or complete forms from home. [Geo-verified audits](/platform/geo-verified-audits) eliminate this failure mode through structural controls:

1. **Geofenced check-in boundaries:** The system calculates the mobile device's live coordinates against the facility's registered coordinates. If the user stands more than 50 metres outside the godown gate, the checklist remains locked.
2. **Native camera hardware lock:** The mobile application directly interfaces with the phone's native camera hardware. Gallery access is disabled at the operating system level, preventing staff from selecting historical screenshots or duplicate photos.
3. **Exif metadata validation:** Every captured photo contains unalterable metadata: UTC timestamp, latitude, longitude, and device orientation.
4. **Randomised question sequence:** The software randomises question clusters each cycle so staff cannot memorise mechanical checkbox taps.

## What should be included in a remote godown inspection checklist?

> **A remote godown inspection checklist must focus on physical asset security, pest ingress, water leakage, material stacking integrity, fire safety readiness, and cash handling procedures. Each checkpoint must require a mandatory live photo, numerical count, or meter reading to eliminate subjective pass marks.**

| Audit Section | Specific Verification Item | Evidence Type Required | Passing Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Perimeter & Security** | Shutter locking bar, CCTV power supply, external floodlights | Live camera photo of lock and DVR monitor | Zero physical damage, 100% cameras online |
| **Material Stacking** | Pallet clearance from walls (minimum 45 cm), stack height limits | Wide-angle photo of primary aisle | No boxes touching walls, stack under 3m |
| **Water & Moisture** | Ceiling joint leakage, damp spots on floor, drain clearance | Close-up photo of high-risk roof corners | Zero moisture stains, drains unobstructed |
| **Pest Control** | Bait station positioning, rodent traps, pest agency log card | Photo of signed monthly vendor card | Card signed within past 30 days |
| **Fire Protection** | Extinguisher pressure gauges, clear 1-metre access zone | Photo of extinguisher pressure needle | Pressure needle in green zone, access clear |
| **Cash & POD Storage** | Safe lock status, physical delivery sheet filing order | Photo of locked locker, POD bundle count | Safe locked, zero unfiled sheets older than 24h |

## How do regional operations teams review remote audits efficiently?

> **Regional managers review remote godown audits through exception-only dashboards that flag failed checkpoints, overdue inspections, and score discrepancies. Instead of reviewing hundreds of passed forms, managers spend their time verifying photographic evidence for flagged safety or inventory hazards.**

Evaluating 80 branches individually each week would overwhelm regional controllers. A modern [operational dashboard](/platform/operational-dashboards) streamlines this workflow:

- **Exception-based sorting:** The central console groups submissions into automated compliance tiers: Grade A (above 90%), Grade B (75% to 89%), and Grade C (under 75%). Any facility with a critical failure (such as blocked fire exits or leaking roofs) immediately triggers an urgent incident ticket.
- **Evidence carousel review:** Regional managers quickly review photographic evidence submitted for red-flagged questions using a split-screen interface, rejecting unsatisfactory corrective actions with a single click.
- **Micro-sampling validations:** Central auditors conduct surprise video or spot-audits on 5% of branches each month to verify that digital self-assessments align with physical realities.

## What happens when a branch fails a remote safety checkpoint?

> **When a branch fails a remote checkpoint, the system automatically creates a corrective action item assigned to the branch manager with a strict completion deadline. The manager must physically fix the hazard, upload fresh photographic proof, and obtain digital approval from the regional manager before the ticket can close.**

Using WhatsApp or email to follow up on godown repairs leads to forgotten tasks and repeated audit failures. A closed-loop digital workflow enforces operational accountability:

```
Branch Submits Audit ---> Extinguisher Expired (Flagged Fail)
                                   |
                                   v
             Automated CAPA Ticket Generated (48h SLA)
                                   |
                                   v
          Branch In-Charge Coordinates Vendor Refill
                                   |
                                   v
          Uploads Geo-Tagged Photo of Newly Tagged Cylinder
                                   |
                                   v
         Regional Safety Head Reviews & Approves in App
                                   |
                                   v
                         Ticket Formally Closed
```

If the 48-hour resolution window expires without verified proof, the ticket automatically escalates to the Zonal Operations Head, preventing local operational issues from lingering unnoticed.
