# Audit Scoring System & Grade Standards

Audiment's scoring engine provides a fair, weighted mathematical assessment of operational quality while ensuring that catastrophic safety breaches are never masked by high scores on routine checklist items.

---

## The Philosophy of Weighted Scoring

In a restaurant or retail setting, having a spotless uniform does not balance out storing raw poultry at room temperature. A traditional unweighted average allows a location with critical safety failures to score 90% simply by acing minor cosmetic items.

Audiment solves this with a **two-tier evaluation model**:
1. **Weighted Question Scoring** (based on operational severity)
2. **Critical Failure Overrides** (automatic grade capping)

---

## 1. Severity Weights & Normalization

Every question in a blueprint is assigned a severity tier with a specific mathematical weight:

| Question Severity | Weight Multiplier | Intended Use Case |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Low** | `1x` | Minor aesthetic, clerical, or procedural standards (e.g., nametag compliance, menu board dusting). |
| **Medium** | `2x` | Operational deficiencies that impact customer experience or efficiency (e.g., service speed, condiment stocking, bathroom supplies). |
| **Critical** | `5x` | **Severe health, safety, fire, or regulatory breaches** (e.g., cross-contamination, food temperature violations, blocked emergency exits). |

### Question Value Calculation
- **Yes / No Questions:**
  - *Yes:* 100% compliance (normalized value: `1.0`)
  - *No:* 0% compliance (normalized value: `0.0`)
  - *Critical No:* Triggers an instant **Critical Failure**.
- **Rating (1–10) Questions:**
  - Rating is normalized linearly (`Rating / 10.0`).
  - *Critical Rating < 6:* Triggers a **Critical Failure**.

## 3. Performance Grades & Critical Overrides

Final percentage scores map to standard operational compliance tiers:

| Performance Tier | Score Range | Operational Meaning | System Action |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1: Excellent** | `≥ 90%` | Outstanding operational execution across all standards. | Logged in high-performance benchmarks. |
| **Tier 2: Good** | `75% – 89%` | Compliant with minor operational drift. | Routine follow-up during next cycle. |
| **Tier 3: Needs Improvement** | `60% – 74%` | Noticeable standards decay across multiple areas. | Manager notified; review recommended. |
| **Tier 4: Sub-Standard** | `< 60%` | Unacceptable operational breakdown. | Immediate escalation to Area Manager. |
| **Critical Failure Override** | *Any Score* | **Triggered if ANY critical question fails.** | **Grade is overridden to Critical Fail regardless of score percentage; automated 48-hour CAPA created.** |

### The Critical Failure Safety Valve
Even if an outlet earns 92% across all other items, failing a single **Critical** question instantly stamps the audit grade as **Critical Fail**. This ensures that headquarters leadership is never misled by a high composite score when an egregious violation exists.

---

## 4. Multi-Audit Trend Detection

Audiment monitors score trajectories across consecutive inspections:
- **Consecutive Failure Alert:** If a location scores below 60% on **3 consecutive audits**, the system flags the branch with an active **Trend Alert**.
- **Trend Banners:** Warning banners appear across Admin and Manager overview dashboards, prompting immediate supervisory visits before health inspectors or customers take notice.
